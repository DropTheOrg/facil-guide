## What you will learn

In this guide, you will learn how to make the text on your iPhone bigger and easier to read. If you find yourself squinting at small words on your screen, this simple change will make a real difference. You can adjust the size until it feels comfortable for your eyes.

## What you need

- An iPhone (any model)
- About two minutes of your time

## The steps

### Step 1: Open Settings

Go to your home screen and tap the **Settings** app. It has a grey icon that looks like gears. This is where you control how your iPhone looks and behaves.

### Step 2: Go to Display & Brightness

Scroll down the Settings list and tap **Display & Brightness**. This section lets you control how your screen looks, including the size of the text.

### Step 3: Tap Text Size

Inside Display & Brightness, you will see an option called **Text Size**. Tap on it. You will see a slider at the bottom of the screen with a small letter "A" on the left and a large letter "A" on the right.

### Step 4: Drag the slider to make text bigger

Put your finger on the **slider** and drag it to the right to make the text bigger. As you drag, you will see the sample text above the slider change size in real time. Stop when the size feels comfortable and easy to read. The change is applied immediately -- you do not need to press a save button.

## Helpful tips

- **Bold Text makes words clearer**: Go back to Display & Brightness and turn on **Bold Text**. This makes all the words on your screen thicker and easier to read, which helps a lot alongside bigger text.
- **You can always change it back**: If the text becomes too big, just go back to the same slider and drag it to the left to make it smaller again.
- **Try it out**: After changing the size, open a few apps like Messages or Safari to see how the new size looks in everyday use.
