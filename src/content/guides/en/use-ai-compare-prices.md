## What you will learn

In this guide, you will learn how to use AI to compare products and prices before making a purchase. AI can help you understand the differences between products, list pros and cons, and narrow down your choices. However, it does not have real-time price data, so you must always check prices on actual store websites.

**Important:** AI does not have real-time price data. Always check the actual store website before buying anything.

## What you need

- A computer, tablet, or smartphone with internet access
- A free ChatGPT account at chat.openai.com
- Access to online stores for price verification

## Step-by-step instructions

### Step 1: Describe what you want to buy

Open ChatGPT and describe what you are looking for. Be specific about your needs and budget:

- "I want to buy a tablet for reading and video calls. My budget is under 300 euros. What are my best options?"
- "I need a simple smartphone for my mother who is 75. She only needs to make calls, send messages, and take photos. What do you recommend?"
- "Compare the Samsung Galaxy A15 and the Xiaomi Redmi Note 13 for someone who mainly uses WhatsApp and email"

### Step 2: Ask for a comparison with pros and cons

Once AI suggests some options, ask for a detailed comparison:

- "Compare those three tablets. List the pros and cons of each."
- "Which of those phones has the biggest screen and easiest interface for a senior?"
- "Put the comparison in a simple table format"

ChatGPT will organize the information so you can see the differences clearly.

### Step 3: Ask specific questions about features

If you are unsure about certain features, ask:

- "What does RAM mean and how much do I need for basic use?"
- "Is 64GB of storage enough for photos and apps?"
- "Does this phone work with hearing aids?"
- "Which one has a better camera in good light?"

ChatGPT will explain technical terms in plain language.

### Step 4: Ask about where to buy

You can ask ChatGPT for general buying advice:

- "Where can I buy this phone in Portugal?"
- "Is it usually cheaper to buy online or in a store?"
- "What should I check before buying a refurbished phone?"

Remember that AI cannot check current stock or current promotions.

### Step 5: Verify prices on real store websites

Before making any purchase, go to real store websites to check:

- The current price (it may be different from what AI said)
- Whether the product is in stock
- Shipping costs and delivery time
- Return and warranty policies
- Real customer reviews

Good places to check include Amazon, the manufacturer's website, and your local electronics store's website.

## Tips

- **Use AI for understanding, stores for buying.** AI is great at explaining differences between products. Actual stores have the real prices.
- **Ask AI to explain jargon.** If a product listing has technical terms you do not understand, paste them into ChatGPT and ask "What does this mean in simple terms?"
- **Check multiple sources.** Do not rely on AI alone. Read reviews from real users on Amazon, specialized review websites, or consumer organizations.
- **Be wary of AI brand preferences.** AI may recommend certain brands more than others simply because they appear more often in its training data, not because they are better.
