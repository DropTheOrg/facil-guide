## What you will learn

In this guide, you will learn how to ask ChatGPT to explain any topic in plain, simple language. Whether you want to understand a medical term, a news headline, or a technology concept, ChatGPT can break it down for you in a way that is easy to follow.

## What you need

- A computer, tablet, or smartphone with internet access
- A web browser (Safari, Chrome, Firefox, or Edge)
- A free ChatGPT account at chat.openai.com

## Step-by-step instructions

### Step 1: Open ChatGPT

Open your web browser and go to **chat.openai.com**. Sign in with your account. If you do not have an account yet, you can create one for free using your email address.

Once logged in, you will see a text box at the bottom of the screen where you can type your message.

### Step 2: Type your question clearly

In the text box, type what you want to understand. Be specific about what you need explained. For example:

- "What is cholesterol?"
- "What does inflation mean?"
- "How does a VPN work?"

Press **Enter** or tap the send button.

### Step 3: Ask ChatGPT to simplify its answer

If the first answer uses words you do not understand, ask ChatGPT to make it simpler. You can type:

- "Can you explain that in simpler words?"
- "Explain it like I am 10 years old"
- "I do not understand the part about [topic]. Can you explain just that part?"

ChatGPT will rewrite its answer using easier language.

### Step 4: Ask follow-up questions

You can keep asking questions in the same conversation. ChatGPT remembers what you were talking about. For example:

- First question: "What is diabetes?"
- Follow-up: "What foods should a person with diabetes avoid?"
- Another follow-up: "Is diabetes curable?"

Just type your next question and send it.

### Step 5: Save or copy the answer

If you want to keep the explanation, you can:

- **Copy the text**: Select the text with your mouse or finger, then right-click and choose "Copy" (or press Ctrl+C on a computer).
- **Take a screenshot**: Press the Print Screen button on your keyboard, or use the screenshot feature on your phone.
- **Bookmark the conversation**: ChatGPT saves your conversations in the sidebar on the left. You can return to them later.

## Tips

- **Be specific.** Instead of asking "Tell me about health," try "What is high blood pressure and why is it dangerous?"
- **Ask for examples.** If a concept is hard to grasp, say "Can you give me an everyday example?"
- **Do not be afraid to ask again.** There is no limit to how many times you can ask ChatGPT to explain something differently.
- **Check important information.** ChatGPT is helpful but not always correct. For medical, legal, or financial topics, always confirm with a professional.
