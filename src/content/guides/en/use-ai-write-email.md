## What you will learn

In this guide, you will learn how to use an AI tool like ChatGPT to help you write emails. You describe what you want to say, the AI writes a draft, and you review and edit it before sending. This is useful when you are not sure how to word a message or when you want to save time.

**Important:** Always read the email before sending it. AI can sometimes add things you did not mean to say.

## What you need

- A computer, tablet, or smartphone with internet access
- A web browser (Safari, Chrome, Firefox, or Edge)
- A free account on ChatGPT (chat.openai.com) or another AI tool
- Access to your email app or webmail

## Step-by-step instructions

### Step 1: Open ChatGPT

Open your web browser and go to **chat.openai.com**. Sign in with your account. You will see a text box at the bottom of the screen.

### Step 2: Describe what you want to say

In the text box, explain what your email should say. Include who it is for and the purpose. For example:

- "Write a polite email to my doctor to cancel my appointment on Thursday"
- "Write a short email to my neighbour thanking them for watching my cat"
- "Write a formal email to customer service asking for a refund on my order"

The more detail you give, the better the result. Press **Enter** to send.

### Step 3: Read the draft carefully

ChatGPT will write a complete email for you. Read it from beginning to end. Check that:

- The information is correct
- The tone feels right (not too casual, not too formal)
- It says what you actually want to say
- There are no details that you did not provide (AI sometimes invents facts)

### Step 4: Ask for changes if needed

If something is not right, ask ChatGPT to fix it. For example:

- "Make the tone more friendly"
- "Remove the part about the weekend"
- "Add that I need a response by Friday"
- "Make it shorter"

ChatGPT will rewrite the email with your changes.

### Step 5: Copy the email

Once you are happy with the draft, copy the text. Select all the text with your mouse or finger, then right-click and choose "Copy" (or press Ctrl+C on a computer).

### Step 6: Paste into your email app and review before sending

Open your email app (Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo Mail, or whatever you use). Start a new email, fill in the recipient's address and subject line, then paste the text into the body of the email (right-click and "Paste" or press Ctrl+V).

**Read it one more time before you press Send.** Make any final changes you want. Add your name at the bottom if the AI did not include it.

## Tips

- **Start simple.** You do not need to write a perfect prompt. Just describe the situation in plain words.
- **Ask for different versions.** If you do not like the first draft, say "Try a different approach" or "Write another version."
- **Never send without reading.** AI sometimes adds facts or details that are not true. Always check.
- **Add a personal touch.** Change a word or two to make it sound more like you.
