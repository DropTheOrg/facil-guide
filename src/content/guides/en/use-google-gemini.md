## What you will learn

In this guide, you will learn how to use Google Gemini, which is Google's AI assistant. Like ChatGPT, it can answer questions, help you write messages, explain things in simple terms, and much more. If you already have a Google account, you can start using it right away.

## What you need

- A computer, tablet, or smartphone with internet access
- A web browser (Safari, Chrome, Firefox, or Edge)
- A Google account (the same one you use for Gmail or YouTube)

## Step-by-step instructions

### Step 1: Go to Google Gemini

Open your web browser and go to **gemini.google.com**. You will see the Gemini homepage with a button to sign in.

### Step 2: Sign in with your Google account

Click **Sign in** and enter your Google email address and password. This is the same account you use for Gmail, YouTube, or Google Maps.

If you do not have a Google account, you will need to create one first. Click "Create account" and follow the instructions.

Once signed in, you will see a text box where you can start typing your questions.

### Step 3: Ask your first question

In the text box at the bottom of the screen, type a question or request. For example:

- "What is the difference between a virus and bacteria?"
- "Give me a simple recipe for tomato soup"
- "Help me write a thank you note for a wedding gift"

Press **Enter** or tap the send button. Gemini will write its answer on the screen.

### Step 4: Have a conversation

Like ChatGPT, Gemini remembers what you discussed in the same conversation. You can ask follow-up questions without repeating the context:

- First question: "What are good exercises for seniors?"
- Follow-up: "Which of those can I do at home?"
- Another: "How many minutes per day should I exercise?"

### Step 5: Try using Gemini with images

Gemini can analyze images. You can upload a photo and ask questions about it. Click the image icon in the text box and select a photo from your device. Then ask:

- "What plant is this?"
- "Can you read the text in this photo?"
- "What breed is this dog?"

This feature is useful for identifying things you see in everyday life.

### Step 6: Start a new conversation

When you want to talk about a different topic, click **New chat** in the sidebar on the left. This starts a fresh conversation. Your previous conversations are saved and you can return to them later by clicking on them in the sidebar.

## Tips

- **Google integration.** Gemini can work with other Google services. You can ask it to help you draft emails for Gmail or plan events for Google Calendar.
- **Compare with ChatGPT.** If you already use ChatGPT, try asking Gemini the same questions and see which answers you prefer. Different AI tools have different strengths.
- **Gemini on your phone.** You can also use Gemini on your smartphone through the web browser or by downloading the Gemini app from the App Store or Google Play Store.
- **Check answers.** Like all AI tools, Gemini can sometimes give incorrect information. Always verify important facts with other sources.
