## What you will learn

In this guide, you will learn how to use AI to help plan a trip. AI can suggest destinations, build day-by-day itineraries, recommend activities, and estimate costs. However, you should always verify its suggestions independently.

**Important:** Always verify AI suggestions. AI can invent restaurants, hotels, or attractions that do not exist. Check everything on Google Maps or a booking website before making plans.

## What you need

- A computer, tablet, or smartphone with internet access
- A free ChatGPT account at chat.openai.com
- Access to booking websites for verification (Google Maps, Booking.com, TripAdvisor)

## Step-by-step instructions

### Step 1: Ask for destination ideas

Start by telling ChatGPT what kind of trip you want. Be specific about your preferences:

- "I want a relaxing beach holiday in Europe in October for under 1500 euros. What destinations do you suggest?"
- "Where can I go for a week of sightseeing that is safe and easy for someone in their 70s?"
- "Suggest three warm destinations with good food that are easy to reach from London"

The more details you give, the better the suggestions will be.

### Step 2: Request a day-by-day itinerary

Once you have chosen a destination, ask ChatGPT to create an itinerary:

- "Create a 5-day itinerary for Lisbon. I walk slowly and prefer quiet places. Include lunch and dinner suggestions."
- "Plan a week in Barcelona for a couple who likes history, food, and gardens. Not too rushed."

ChatGPT will write a detailed plan for each day, including morning, afternoon, and evening activities.

### Step 3: Ask about costs

You can ask ChatGPT for general cost estimates:

- "How much does a typical meal cost in Lisbon?"
- "What is the average hotel price per night in Barcelona in October?"
- "Is it cheaper to take the train or fly from Paris to Barcelona?"

Remember that these are estimates, not real-time prices. Use them as a rough guide only.

### Step 4: Ask for restaurant and hotel suggestions

ChatGPT can suggest places to eat and stay:

- "Recommend three traditional restaurants near the city center of Lisbon"
- "What are some good, moderately priced hotels in Barcelona near public transport?"

Write down the names it gives you.

### Step 5: Verify everything independently

This is the most important step. For every suggestion AI gives you:

- Search the restaurant or hotel name on **Google Maps** to confirm it exists
- Check reviews on **TripAdvisor** or **Booking.com**
- Verify opening hours and current prices on the establishment's own website
- Confirm that attractions are actually open during your travel dates

Do not book anything based solely on AI recommendations.

### Step 6: Ask for practical travel tips

ChatGPT can also help with practical questions:

- "What is the best way to get from the airport to the city center in Lisbon?"
- "Do I need to tip in restaurants in Spain?"
- "What should I pack for Barcelona in October?"
- "Are there any common tourist scams I should know about?"

These tips are generally reliable but still worth double-checking for important details.

## Tips

- **Use AI as a starting point, not a final plan.** AI is great for inspiration and rough planning, but always verify the details yourself.
- **Ask for alternatives.** If a suggestion does not appeal to you, say "Give me different options" or "Something less touristy."
- **Save the conversation.** ChatGPT saves your conversations so you can return to your travel plan later and add more details.
- **Combine AI with travel websites.** Use AI for ideas and itineraries, then use TripAdvisor, Google Maps, and Booking.com for verification and bookings.
